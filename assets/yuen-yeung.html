Spec Sheet: "Yuen Yeung" (6oz)

Yield: 1 x 6oz Beverage
Espresso: 1 Shot only (~18-20g)
Milk tea per drink: ~5oz, steamed
Batch cap: 1 gallon, about 25 drinks
Per Drink:
- 1 Shot Espresso (~18-20g)
- 1 Pump Simple Syrup
- ~5oz Batched black-tea milk

Tea: Black only. Ceylon, or a Taiwan black (hong cha / Assam-style). Not green.

Night-Before Prep: 1 Gallon Maximum

This is the same idea as the old chai concentrate: brew the tea hot, strain it, then build drinks off the batch. The difference is the tea goes into the lactose-free milk the night before, and you steam that milk tea — you do not steam plain milk on top of a separate chai shot.

Do not bloom the leaves, and do not stuff dry tea into the cold carton overnight. Blooming is a coffee move. A short wet of black tea in cold milk does not extract. Leaves left in the carton overnight go from weak to bitter, and a carton of hot milk sealed and left to cool spends too long in the danger zone.

  1. Measure 40g black tea (Ceylon or Taiwan black). Not green.
  2. Bring 1 quart of water (~950ml) to a rolling boil.
  3. Pour the boiling water over the leaves. Stir once. Steep 6 minutes. Set a timer.
  4. Strain hard through a fine mesh and press the leaves. Discard the leaves. Expect about 800-850ml of concentrate after the leaves drink their share.
  5. Cool the concentrate until it is no longer steaming. An ice bath is fine. It must be cool before it touches the milk.
  6. From a 1-gallon carton of lactose-free milk, pour off about 1 quart of plain milk and save it for other drinks. Pour the cooled concentrate back into the carton to replace that volume.
  7. Cap the carton. Invert it gently to mix. Refrigerate overnight.
  8. Leaves are already out. Morning service does not start with a strain. If fines remain, pass the carton once through a fine mesh before it goes on the line.

Hold: Service day only. Dump whatever is left at close. Do not keep milk tea a second day. Never prep more than 1 gallon. One gallon at ~5oz per drink is about 25 drinks.

Standard Operating Procedure (Per Drink)

  1. Pull the shot: Pull 1 shot of espresso (~18-20g) into an 6oz cup. Single shot only.
  2. Sweeten and agitate: Add 1 pump of simple syrup into the espresso and swirl until the syrup is dissolved in the shot.
  3. Steam the milk tea: Purge the steam wand. Steam about 5oz of the batched milk tea in a small pitcher to a wet-paint microfoam, the same texture as a flat white. Do not scald it. Lactose-free milk splits if you boil it.
  4. Pour: Pour a 3-layer tulip. One, two, three, and cut.

Professional Notes

  • Why black, not green: Yuen yeung is coffee plus Hong Kong milk tea. That tea is black. Green tea goes vegetal and bitter under a shot of espresso, and it will not taste like the drink.
  • Why the leaves come out before the fridge: The overnight rest is for the brewed milk tea to chill and settle. It is not a second steep. Leaving leaves in the carton keeps extracting tannin into dairy.
  • Single shot only: This is an 6oz drink built on a gallon batch. A double shot throws off the coffee-to-tea balance this recipe is written for.
