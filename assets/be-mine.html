Spec Sheet: "Be Mine" Matcha Latte (6oz, Hot Only)

Yield: 1 x 6oz Beverage
Service: Hot only. No iced build.
Temp Target: 170°F - 180°F
Whisk Time: ~30 Seconds
Ingredients:
- 3.0g Sifted Matcha
- 30.0g Water
- 1 Pump Yuzu Syrup (Optional)
- ~5oz Milk (Whole, 2%, or Lactose-Free)

Batch Prep for Service

Matcha does not hold overnight. Do not steep it in milk, and do not whisk a concentrate the night before.

  1. Pre-portion: Before service, sift matcha and dose 3.0g into dry cups or a sealed tin. The line should not be sifting mid-rush.
  2. Same-service concentrate only: If a rush is coming, whisk concentrates at the start of service and use them that service. Matcha settles and dulls if it sits.
  3. Dump at close: Any whisked matcha left at the end of service goes out. Tomorrow starts from dry powder.

Standard Operating Procedure

  1. Prep the Whisk: Fill a cup with hot water and submerge the tines of the bamboo whisk to soften them, which prevents the delicate bamboo from snapping during use.
  2. Sift & Dose: Place your bowl on a scale. Sift exactly 3.0g of matcha through a fine mesh sieve into the bowl, or use a pre-portioned 3.0g dose from service prep. Skipping the sift guarantees a gritty, clumpy drink.
  3. Water Addition: Tare the scale and pour exactly 30.0g of 170°F-180°F water over the powder.
  4. Whisking Technique: Hold the whisk comfortably and use your wrist—not your arm—to aggressively whisk in a "W" or "M" motion. Hover the whisk slightly above the bottom to avoid grinding the tines. Whisk for about 30 seconds.
  5. Surface Correction: Slowly lift the whisk toward the surface while continuing the motion to break up any large surface bubbles, leaving behind a smooth microfoam.
  6. Build the Base: Pour the matcha concentrate and 1 pump of yuzu syrup into the serving cup. Visual cue of a properly done matcha: a smooth, fully suspended liquid with dense microfoam and zero dry powder left in your mixing bowl.
  7. Milk Steaming: Purge your steam wand. Steam your milk in a 12oz pitcher to a wet-paint microfoam texture, identical to flat white standards.
  8. Pour: Integrate the milk into the matcha base slowly, then drop close to the surface and pour the house pattern: a 3-layer tulip. One, two, three, and cut. This drink is hot only.

Professional Pointers & Scaling

  • Scaling for Two (two 6oz drinks): If you need to make two drinks at once, do not simply double the dose in a small bowl. You must use a large, commercial-width chawan to accommodate 6.0g of matcha and 60.0g of water so the whisk has room to aerate the liquid properly. If your bowl is too small, you must whisk them sequentially.
  • Why Sifting Matters: Matcha does not dissolve; it suspends in water. Un-sifted matcha will instantly lock into dry clumps when water hits it, ruining the texture.
  • Hot only: Do not build this over ice. The recipe is the hot 6oz latte above.
