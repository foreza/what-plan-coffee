Spec Sheet: Mocha (6oz, Hot Only)

Yield: 1 x 6oz Beverage
Espresso: 1 Shot (~18-20g)
Service: Hot only
Ingredients:
- 3 Guittard Organic Bittersweet Chocolate Baking Wafers
- 1 Pump Simple Syrup
- 1 Shot Espresso (~18-20g)
- ~4.5-5oz Milk, steamed (2% lactose-free)

Standard Operating Procedure

  1. Wafers in the cup: Put 3 Guittard Organic Bittersweet Chocolate Baking Wafers in the 6oz cup.
  2. Shot onto the chocolate: Pull 1 shot of espresso (~18-20g) directly onto the wafers.
  3. Agitate with the espresso: Add 1 pump of simple syrup. Stir until the wafers are fully melted into the shot. This agitation happens now, before any milk. If a wafer is still solid, keep stirring. Do not bury chunks under milk.
  4. Steam the milk: Purge the steam wand. Steam about 4.5-5oz of 2% lactose-free milk to a wet-paint microfoam.
  5. Pour: Pour to 6oz. House pattern: a 3-layer tulip. One, two, three, and cut.

Professional Notes

  • Melt it in the shot: The chocolate has to be gone before the milk goes in. Milk poured onto a solid wafer will not finish the melt, and the drink will be grainy.
  • Hot chocolate is not this drink with more chocolate: Hot chocolate uses 6 wafers and is agitated with a splash of milk, because there is no espresso to melt them. Do not swap the two methods.
  • Milk: 2% lactose-free is the default. Oat milk is available on request.
