Spec Sheet: Hot Chocolate (6oz, Hot Only)

Yield: 1 x 6oz Beverage
Espresso: None
Service: Hot only
Ingredients:
- 6 Guittard Organic Bittersweet Chocolate Baking Wafers
- 1 Pump Simple Syrup
- ~6oz Milk, steamed (2% lactose-free)

Standard Operating Procedure

  1. Steam first: Purge the steam wand. Steam about 6oz of 2% lactose-free milk to a wet-paint microfoam. You need the milk hot before the chocolate can melt.
  2. Cup: Put 6 Guittard Organic Bittersweet Chocolate Baking Wafers in the 6oz cup. Add 1 pump of simple syrup. That is double the mocha dose. There is no espresso in this drink.
  3. Agitate with milk: Pour in a small splash of the hot steamed milk, enough to cover the wafers. Stir until the chocolate is fully melted. This is the agitation step. Do it before the rest of the milk goes in.
  4. Finish the pour: Pour the rest of the milk to 6oz. House pattern: a 3-layer tulip. One, two, three, and cut.
  5. If the base is too thick for the layers to show: Thin with a touch more milk and pour again. Do not skip the art.

Professional Notes

  • Melt it in milk, not in a shot: There is no espresso here. A splash of hot milk is what dissolves the six wafers. If you pour the full pitcher in at once, the wafers sit on the bottom.
  • Mocha is the other method: Mocha is 3 wafers, melted in a single shot of espresso, then milk. Do not use that order for hot chocolate.
  • Milk: 2% lactose-free is the default. Oat milk is available on request.
